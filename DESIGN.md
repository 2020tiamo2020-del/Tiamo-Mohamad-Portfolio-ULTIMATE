\# Design System \& Guidelines

\- \*\*Visual Style:\*\* Clean, modern, cinematic, and dark-themed minimalist portfolio layout tailored for digital creators.

\- \*\*Color Palette:\*\* High contrast, deep dark backgrounds combined with vibrant accents to emphasize visual media assets.

\- \*\*Typography:\*\* Clear, readable sans-serif fonts optimized for both desktop and mobile screens.

\- \*\*Layout Principles:\*\* Responsive grid systems, smooth transitions, high-resolution imagery presentation without compression artifacts, and distraction-free navigation.
